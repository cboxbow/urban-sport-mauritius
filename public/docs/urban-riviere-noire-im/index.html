The West Coast Development Story

The asset sits inside Mauritius' most attractive lifestyle corridor.

Riviere Noire and Tamarin are no longer secondary leisure zones. They have become a concentration point for premium residences, golf-linked communities, marina living, expatriate households, international visitors, and hospitality-linked consumption.

  • Cap Tamarin & Black Rock
    Large-scale mixed-use residential momentum anchored by affluent residents and modern lifestyle infrastructure.
  • Tamarina & Harmonie Golf
    Golf, villas, and high-spending households reinforcing premium leisure demand across the west.
  • La Balise Marina & Les Salines
    Waterfront and coastal living that support destination appeal and discretionary consumption.
  • Plantation Marguery & IRS / PDS stock
    A recurring base of owners, expatriates, and second-home users aligned with premium club usage.
West coast context
